Our Engagement Functions

A joyous daytime celebration following sacred Sagai traditions — from Atithi Swagat and auspicious Tilak to the Ring Ceremony, Sangeet celebrations, and Shahi Bhoj.

✦ 💖 ✦
Function 01 · Swagat 10:30 AM onwards
💐 Atithi Swagat & Floral Greeting

Swagat & Refreshments

Guests arrive to refreshing thandai, artisanal mocktails, and morning delicacies. Shivam warmly presents a gorgeous flower bouquet to Kajal with affectionate greetings as both families exchange joyful welcomes and introduce extended loved ones.

📅 Date: Sunday, 13 December 2026
👗 Dress Code: Sophisticated morning pastels & floral elegance
Function 02 · Main Sagai Rituals 11:45 AM
💍 Ganesh Puja, Tilak & Ring Exchange

Puja, Tilak & Ring Ceremony

Seeking divine blessings with Ganesh Puja, followed by the auspicious Tilak & Chunni Chadhai ceremony, reading of the sacred Lagna Patrika, Jai Mala garland exchange, and Shivam & Kajal exchanging diamond engagement rings with sweet feeding (mithai).

📅 Date: Sunday, 13 December 2026
👗 Dress Code: Traditional festive ethnic & royal pastels
Function 03 · Blessings & Music 1:00 PM onwards
🎶 Shagun, Aashirwad & Sangeet

Shagun, Aashirwad & Sangeet

Exchange of ceremonial Shagun hampers, touching elders' feet for their blessings with unbroken rice (akshat) and flower showers, leading into upbeat family dance performances, cousin choreography, and celebratory music.

📅 Date: Sunday, 13 December 2026
👗 Dress Code: Celebratory designer ethnic wear
Function 04 · Grand Feast 1:45 PM – 4:30 PM
🍽️ Shahi Bhoj (Grand Lunch)

Shahi Bhoj & Royal Feast

A lavish daytime gastronomic banquet featuring royal Awadhi & Mughlai delicacies, Mumbai street chaat live counters, aromatic biryanis, and traditional desserts like piping hot jalebi-rabri, kheer, and gajar ka halwa.

📅 Date: Sunday, 13 December 2026
👗 Dress Code: Royal festive elegance
